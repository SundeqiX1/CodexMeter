# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing credentials or private account data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, credential collection, or deceptive binaries.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting feature. Maintainers may remove content, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant depending on severity and context. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.
